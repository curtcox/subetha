Edge lab

Move messages between two devices with no network, using only the speaker and microphone, the screen and camera, or Bluetooth. Nothing reaches the relay, so it’s free. Open this page on two devices and set the same topic on both. Messages are signed with a key generated in this tab. The SDK behind this page is /edge.js (docs).

Sound

16-FSK at 2–5 kHz, about 50 bytes a second. Turn the volume up and keep the devices within a metre or two.

Animated QR

Fountain-coded frames: the camera can join late and miss frames, and it still finishes.

Bluetooth LE

Connects to a nearby phone running a SubEtha app (browsers can only be the central). The two then swap messages by gossip, store and forward.
