# SubEtha Acceptable Use Policy (DRAFT)

_Last updated: [DATE]. Draft for legal review._

You may not use SubEtha to:

1. Send messages to people who haven't agreed to receive them (spam), including bulk unsolicited
   email, SMS, or calls.
2. Send content that is illegal, harassing, threatening, fraudulent, or that infringes others'
   rights; or malware, phishing, or credential-collection pages.
3. Generate artificial traffic to premium or high-cost numbers ("SMS pumping", toll fraud), or try
   to make the Service spend money it isn't paid for.
4. Evade rate limits, spend caps, verification, or other controls, including by creating many
   accounts or tokens.
5. Interfere with the Service, other users, or connected networks (including Reticulum and
   Bluetooth meshes), for example by flooding topics or gossip.
6. Use the Service as the sole means of life-safety or emergency notification.

## SMS and voice (US A2P 10DLC)

SubEtha sends SMS and calls only to **numbers verified by their owner** with a one-time code.
If you route messages to phone numbers, you confirm that:

- each recipient opted in to receive messages about the topic they subscribed to, and you can show
  that consent on request;
- messages are transactional or alert notifications the recipient asked for, not marketing;
- recipients can opt out at any time by replying **STOP** (we stop all messages to that number
  immediately and keep a record), and get help by replying **HELP**.

Consent language shown when a number is verified:

> By entering this code, you agree to receive automated notification texts and calls from SubEtha
> for the topics you've subscribed to. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply
> STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See subetha.org/terms and subetha.org/privacy.

HELP reply text:

> SubEtha notifications. Manage at subetha.org/console. Reply STOP to opt out. Support: [CONTACT EMAIL].

International SMS and voice are disabled by default and enabled per country only after review.

## Email
Outbound email goes only to addresses verified by their owner. Every message identifies the topic
and links to the console to stop delivery. Inbound email to `…@in.subetha.org` is accepted only
from senders the topic owner allows.

## Enforcement
We may remove content, revoke tokens, block destinations, pause topics, or suspend accounts that
violate this policy, and report illegal activity to authorities. Report abuse to [ABUSE EMAIL].
